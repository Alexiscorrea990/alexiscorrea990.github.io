Teaching & mentoring

Helping students move from memorizing chemistry to reasoning with it.

My teaching spans general chemistry, cell biology, physiology, laboratory instruction, assessment design, and small-group problem solving.

“Good scientific training builds confidence without sacrificing rigor—and gives students the tools to ask better questions.”

As a Burg Teaching Fellow and teaching assistant at USC, I have worked with students in lecture, laboratory, office-hour, and problem-solving settings. I aim to make complex ideas approachable while keeping the reasoning visible.

Approach

Principles that guide my teaching

01

Reason from evidence

I emphasize how observations support conclusions, not only which answer is correct.

02

Make thinking visible

Stepwise problem solving helps students identify where understanding breaks down.

03

Connect scales

I link molecular interactions to experimental readouts and biological consequences.

04

Build scientific agency

Students learn best when they see themselves as capable investigators, not passive recipients.
