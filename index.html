💡 What is monero-web-wallet?

monero-web-wallet is a self-hosted, non-custodial Monero (XMR) web wallet that puts you in total control of your cryptocurrency. Built with a sleek dark responsive interface, it runs on top of the official monero-wallet-rpc — the gold standard in Monero wallet technology.

This means your private keys remain on your machine at all times. No third parties, no servers, no intermediaries. Just you, your funds, and the privacy-focused Monero blockchain. Whether you're new to crypto or a seasoned user, this wallet is designed to be simple yet powerful.

⚙️ How It Works (Simple Technical Overview)

For the curious, monero-web-wallet acts as a bridge between your browser and the Monero network. Here's a simplified breakdown:

  • Frontend (What you see): A sleek interface built with React and Tailwind CSS, served directly to your browser.
  • Backend (The brains): A Node.js server (using Express) that communicates securely with the Monero daemon.
  • The Core (Monero-Wallet-RPC): The official Monero software handles all transaction logic and key management locally.
  • JSON-RPC: The secure communication protocol between the frontend and the backend.

This architecture ensures that even though you access the wallet through a web interface, everything runs locally on your machine. You get browser convenience without sacrificing security.
